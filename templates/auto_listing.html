Automate your e-commerce listings upload process. Once you generate and download your promotional image bundles, use our custom Google Chrome Extension to auto-publish listings directly to Meesho.

Step-by-Step Installation Guide

Takes 2 Mins
1

Download the Extension Bundle

Click the "Download Extension ZIP" button in the panel on the right. This will download the latest release of our listing helper extension named jenn_auto_listing.zip.

2

Extract the ZIP Archive

Locate the downloaded ZIP file in your file explorer. Right-click and choose "Extract All" or use software like WinRAR/7-Zip to extract the contents to a folder (e.g., jenn auto listing).

3

Open Chrome Extensions Settings

Launch Google Chrome (or Edge/Brave), open a new tab, and enter chrome://extensions/ in the URL search bar. Alternatively, click the puzzle icon in the toolbar and select "Manage Extensions".

4

Enable Developer Mode

In the top-right corner of the Extensions dashboard, locate the **"Developer Mode"** toggle switch and turn it **ON**.

5

Load Unpacked Extension Folder

Click on the **"Load unpacked"** button that appeared in the top-left menu bar. Browse your files, select the extracted jenn auto listing folder, and click "Select Folder".

6

Extension Loaded & Ready

The auto-listing helper is now active. Click the puzzle extension icon in Chrome's top bar, locate "Jenn Auto Listing", pin it, and start publishing bulk product listings instantly.
